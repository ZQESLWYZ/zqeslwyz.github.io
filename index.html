Research Projects

CalibGS — Calibrated Selective Geometric Completion

WACV 20273D Gaussian Splatting

Spending primitives only where multi-view evidence supports them.

  • Recast 3D Gaussian Splatting densification as a calibrated three-action decision — free propagation, surface-constrained completion, or rejection — instead of reading every unexplained image residual as evidence for new geometry.
  • Built the full risk pipeline: multi-view evidence features (track support, triangulation geometry, reprojection consistency, gradient agreement, local planar structure), an MLP predictor for the early held-out-view damage event, and a monotone isotonic calibration map fitted on a scene-disjoint split, leaving only the two risk thresholds tunable.
  • On sparse-view DTU (24 input views) under a matched 0.20M-Gaussian budget, reached 24.18 dB PSNR and 0.694 mm Chamfer distance, cutting Chamfer by 31.4% and depth inconsistency by 36.6% relative to GaussianPro, and outperformed 3DGS, 2DGS, and Sparse2DGS on DTU, Replica, Tanks and Temples, and Mip-NeRF 360.
  • Showed that the gate measures geometric failure rather than image error alone: 0.836 AUROC at 0.024 ECE, versus 0.684 AUROC and 0.118 ECE for a residual-only score, with ablations separating the contributions of calibration, rejection, and the planar constraint.

Nexus — Structured Synergy for Efficient Text-to-Image Generation

ACCV 2026Rectified Flow & Efficient Generation

Competitive quality at a fraction of the activated compute.

  • Designed Nexus, a rectified-flow text-to-image framework that jointly optimizes three levers usually studied in isolation: sparse MoE-FFN layers for capacity at low activated cost, Gated DeltaNet linear attention for near-linear scaling with sequence length, and expert-level low-bit quantization-aware training.
  • Set the block layout (six dual-stream layers followed by single-stream blocks, CLIP-G/14 and T5-XXL conditioning, no added cross-attention) and the per-expert INT4 scaling scheme that keeps the router and state matrix in FP16, where uniform global INT4 degrades FID from 5.8 to 7.9.
  • A 7B-parameter model activates only 1.6B parameters: FID 5.8 and CLIP score 0.329 on COCO-30K, 1.42 s per 512×512 image at 3.2 GB peak memory and 185 GFLOPs on a single A100 — about 2.8× faster and 1.7× lighter than SD3-Medium.
  • Kept prompt following near the large-model tier under a common INT4 budget (GenEval 0.61, DPG-bench 82.47, ahead of SD3-Medium-INT4 and FLUX.1-INT4 in latency), and scaled to 1024×1024 and 2048×2048 (11.2 s, 12.5 GB) where quadratic attention becomes prohibitively slow or runs out of memory.

Local-to-Global Metric Depth Calibration

Jan. 2026 – Mar. 2026Monocular Depth

Turning relative depth into metric depth, region by region.

  • Built a boundary-aware region graph combining global affine calibration, selectively activated local residuals, and collapse regularization without camera metadata or sparse depth.
  • With frozen Depth Anything V2-Large features, reduced zero-shot DIODE AbsRel from 0.2660 to 0.2358 and increased δ1 from 0.6680 to 0.7037.

Task-Prototype Guided Flow Matching

Oct. 2025 – Dec. 2025Embodied AI

Learning robot skills from a handful of demonstrations.

  • Designed a few-shot VLA policy using symmetric cross-attention task prototypes, a prototype-adaptive flow prior, gated AdaLN, and episodic support-query training.
  • Built the PyTorch/ODE action-generation pipeline for LEROBOT-SO101; achieved 66.8%, 79.6%, and 82.1% success at 1/4/6 shots and 54.3 ms inference at 10 Hz.
